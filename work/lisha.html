At a glance

One designer, a whole compliance platform

As the sole designer at LISHA.ai (Legal Intelligence System for Handling Assurance), I led the UX/UI strategy for an AI-powered compliance platform serving DPOs, IT auditors and compliance teams across ISO 27001, GDPR, NIS2 and DORA.

Reporting to the CEO, I designed cross-platform solutions (iOS, Android, web) that translated complex compliance workflows into intuitive interfaces, working closely with engineers to balance design fidelity with technical constraints.

Setting up the context

What is Lisha, and why is it needed?

LISHA.ai is an AI compliance assistant that helps Data Protection Officers, IT auditors and compliance teams streamline policy creation and cut research time. It gives tailored guidance for different roles, such as CTOs, DPOs and CISOs, so the advice is precise and actionable.

Regulatory compliance keeps getting more complex, time-consuming and critical. Fast-moving laws like GDPR, NIS2 and DORA, and standards such as ISO 27001, need constant monitoring, interpretation and application, which puts real strain on compliance teams.

Challenge

Starting from zero with the 3P formula

“When you start a project that has never been attempted before, it’s natural to panic and try everything. Trial and error gets you there eventually, but you lose a lot of time. To avoid that, I applied the 3P formula.”

Three questions framed the work: Where should I begin? What should I cover? What makes it different from others?

1

Problem

Compliance teams are overloaded with complex regulations, scattered documents and manual tracking. Applying guidelines is slow, error-prone and expensive, which raises risk and wastes resources.

2

Pain

Overwhelming regulations, fragmented tools, no real-time visibility of changes, black-box AI with no sources, unclear accountability and a high cognitive load.

3

Possibility

An AI assistant that acts as a compliance copilot, simplifying complex documents and giving real-time, trustworthy answers and workflows.

Design learning

What I took away

As the sole product designer, the biggest lesson was to design deeply for diverse roles while embedding the assistant into existing workflows like Slack and Teams. Simplifying compliance information needs clear, contextual guidance, and trust comes from being transparent about how the AI works.

Designing for accessibility, responsiveness and continuous feedback keeps the product relevant. Balancing scale with usability, and keeping a human in the loop for non-deterministic AI behaviour, lets people manage compliance with confidence.
